Frequently Asked Questions

Are the requests actually unlimited?

Absolutely! As our client, you have the freedom to submit as many requests as you wish. There's no cap on the number of tasks you can queue. We'll process each one sequentially, ensuring you receive each task's outcomes before moving on to the next.

How and where are tasks managed?

We have a notion dashboard created for you that has all the tasks, milestones, resources, notes and everything around the project.

Do you provide white labeled services?

Yes, we evaluate if it aligns with what we're doing. Contact us to know more.

Are there any refunds if I don't like the service?

Yes, we do! If you're not happy with the work at any given moment, just send an email to sales@basenine.co saying "give me my money back". You will have the amount refunded in 24-48 hours

How long do website revamps take?

Depending on the number of pages it can take anywhere between 1-3 months.
We will get your homepage live in under one month. Then plan phase 2 for other releases.

What happens if my project scope changes in mid-way?

We understand that priorities can shift, especially in fast-moving industries. For fixed projects, we can issue a revised scope of work (SOW) to account for any new additions or changes. For retainer clients, these adjustments can often be rolled into your ongoing engagement.

How soon can you start?

We typically start within a week, depending on availability. Get in touch to see if we have any available spots at the moment.
