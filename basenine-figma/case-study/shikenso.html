How Shikenzo regained full control of its website with a Basenine Webflow rebuild

Shikenso is a sponsorship analytics platform helping brands measure the real value of digital and esports partnerships. We rebuilt their website into a scalable Webflow foundation that keeps design quality high while giving the team complete editing freedom.

View Live website
Challenge
❌ A beautiful brand trapped inside a fragile website.
Shikenzo had a strong identity. Clean visuals. High quality work. But the site behind it was unstable. Every update felt risky. Every change carried the fear of breaking something important.
❌ The marketing team could not move at the speed they needed.
Launching new pages required developer help. Simple edits took too long. Campaigns slowed down. The website became a bottleneck instead of a growth tool.
❌ The foundation could not support scale.
There was no component system. No consistent structure. No flexibility. Adding new content felt like hacking the site instead of building it.
❌ The brand investment was not being translated into digital performance.
Shikenzo had the right visuals. The right tone. The right market fit. But the website did not support it. The technical side limited what the brand could do.
After
Before
solution
✅ Rebuilt the entire website for speed, stability, and clarity.
We mapped every pain point with their team. Audited the full build. Defined a simple goal. A stable system that lets marketers move fast without fear of breaking the site.
✅ Integrated Shikenzo’s visual identity inside a clean, modular structure.
We used Client First to rebuild every component. Clean sections. Reusable blocks. A flexible layout system that keeps design quality intact while reducing friction for the team.
✅ Migrated everything to Webflow with a future ready setup.
The new build is fast, accessible, optimized, and scalable. Shikenzo can add pages, update layouts, and create campaigns in minutes without waiting on developers.
THE RESULT
Shikenzo now edits their website without fear. Pages go live in minutes. Layouts stay consistent. Visual quality stays premium. The team can run marketing at full speed with a reliable foundation behind them.

What once felt fragile now feels flexible. What once slowed them down now helps them grow.

I’ve worked with Basenine on multiple projects now, and it’s rare to find someone this reliable. The team bridges design and Webflow flawlessly, catches the small details before you do, and makes the entire process faster, smoother, and simply better.

Mickey Switzer
Partner, Simply Labs
